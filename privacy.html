Privacy Policy

Effective 15 August 2026 · Spika (referred to in this Privacy Policy as “Spika”, “we”, or “us”)

This Privacy Policy explains how Spika, a personal expense tracker mobile app, handles information when you use the Spika mobile app distributed on Google Play.

1. Who we are

Spika is operated by an individual developer. Contact: spika.support@gmail.com

2. What Spika is

Spika helps you track personal spending, accounts, categories, and optional precious-metal balances. Features may include voice logging (“Spika Now”), local notifications, market rate lookups, and optional encrypted backup via Google.

3. Information we process

  • Information you enter: transactions, accounts, categories, budgets/income settings, profile display name, optional custom profile photo stored locally, and similar finance data you choose to save.
  • Google account information (if you sign in): basic profile details provided by Google Sign-In (such as name, email, and profile photo URL when available) to identify your session. Drive backup access is requested only when you explicitly enable backup or confirm a restore — not during ordinary sign-in.
  • Device permissions you grant: microphone (for voice entry), notifications, and network access.
  • Technical data on device: app preferences (theme, language, currency, backup settings) stored locally.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and basic app-usage analytics collected through Google Firebase (see Section 10). These are not a copy of your financial ledger.

Spika is designed so your financial records stay on your device by default. We do not operate a Spika-owned cloud database that stores your transactions for us to read.

4. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: primary storage uses a local database and local preferences.
  • Google Drive App Data (optional, explicit opt-in): When you turn on Google Drive backup in Settings (and grant Drive App Data access), Spika uploads an encrypted backup file to the hidden App Data folder associated with your Google account.

    Spika encrypts the backup on your device before uploading it, using a key derived from your App Lock PIN. This means you can restore your backup on a new phone by entering the same PIN. Google cannot derive this key from your account. Restore is user-confirmed and requires the PIN typed on the device — biometrics are not used for backup encryption and cannot restore a backup on a new device. Forgetting your PIN lets you unlock this phone through account-based recovery, but it does not decrypt an existing Drive backup — that still requires the original PIN.

  • Speech to text: Spika Now uses the device speech service. On Android that service may send audio off the device according to the OS / speech-provider policy.
  • Contact and feature polls: messages you submit (message text, optional reply email, locale, and OS) are POSTed to a Google Apps Script webhook that delivers them to us.
  • Market rates: the app may request public market/price data from cdn.jsdelivr.net (World Bank commodity reference data), currency-api.pages.dev (currency exchange rates), and gold-api.com (live metal spot prices). Those requests are for rates, not for uploading your transaction history.

5. How we use information

  • Provide and improve the expense-tracking features you use
  • Sign you in with Google and restore or create encrypted backups
  • Convert speech to text through the device speech services you allow (this may leave the device on Android)
  • Send local notifications you enable
  • Show approximate market prices for supported metals/currencies
  • Detect crashes and understand basic app usage so we can keep Spika working

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal or financial data. We do not share your transaction history with advertisers.

Limited third-party processing happens only as needed to run the app:

  • Google: Sign-In, optional Drive App Data backup/restore, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics
  • Device OS / speech services: if you use microphone features
  • Contact form / feature polls: Google Apps Script webhook (message, optional reply email, locale, OS)
  • Market/price data providers: jsDelivr CDN (World Bank commodity reference data), currency-api.pages.dev (currency exchange rates), and gold-api.com (live metal spot prices)
  • Google Play: distribution and, in the future, paid subscriptions

7. Subscriptions and payments (future)

Spika is free today. We may introduce paid subscriptions or premium features later. If we do, purchases will be processed by Google Play (and, if we expand to other stores later, by that store). We will not charge you without an in-app confirmation. Store payment details are handled by the store, not entered into Spika’s own forms.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Local data remains until you delete it in-app or uninstall Spika.
  • Encrypted Drive backups remain in your Google App Data until removed by restore/overwrite flows, Google account tools, or app controls that clear them.
  • Uninstalling Spika removes local data on that device; it does not automatically erase a Drive backup that already exists in your Google account.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data is retained by Google according to Firebase practices until we delete it from the project. Uninstalling Spika stops further collection from that device.

9. Security

  • App lock: if you set a PIN or enable biometric unlock, Spika blocks access to your financial data in the main app. PIN attempts are rate-limited with an increasing wait after repeated failures, and if the app cannot verify your lock state for any reason (for example, a storage read error), it fails closed and blocks access rather than opening unlocked. The Spika Now home-screen widget does not require the PIN: it is write-only (log a transaction by voice) and does not show balances. The lock still applies when you open the main app.
  • On-device database encryption: your local financial database is encrypted at rest using a randomly generated, device-bound key held in your device's secure hardware-backed storage (Android Keystore). This key is separate from your app-lock PIN and from backup encryption.
  • Backup encryption: see Section 4 above for how Drive backups are encrypted and the limits of that protection.
  • Screenshots and app-switcher previews: Spika does not block screenshots or recent-apps preview thumbnails. Anyone with access to your unlocked device can capture what is on screen.
  • Signed builds: release versions of Spika distributed through app stores are signed with a private release key that is not included in the app's source code.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Protect your device and your Google account with strong locks and up-to-date software.

10. Analytics and crash reporting

Spika uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to keep the app reliable. These services are operated by Google. They do not receive your transaction history, account balances, or other financial records you enter in Spika.

  • Firebase Analytics: automatic app-usage events such as first open and sessions, plus basic device and app information (for example OS version and app version). Spika does not currently log custom events that include the contents of your finances.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash and error reports, including stack traces, device model, OS version, and app version, so we can find and fix bugs. A crash log is not intended to include your ledger; it may include technical details from the failing code path.

Uninstalling Spika stops further collection from that device. Spika does not currently provide an in-app toggle to disable Analytics or Crashlytics.

11. Children

Spika is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the app if you are below that age.

12. International users

Google Play services are required for Google Sign-In and optional Drive backup. The app still works offline without them.

The app may be used in different countries. Because data is primarily stored on your device and optionally in your own Google account, processing generally follows where you and your providers (Google / your device) operate.

13. Your choices

  • Use Spika without Google Sign-In (local-only features)
  • Disable backup, notifications, or microphone access in system/app settings
  • Sign out of Google from Settings
  • Forgot your app-lock PIN? Use the in-app PIN recovery flow, which checks that the device is still signed in to the same Google account before allowing a PIN reset. This confirms account access on the device, not a separate identity check. Resetting the PIN does not decrypt an existing Drive backup — those files still need the old PIN.
  • Delete your data: use the in-app “Delete all data” control (Settings) to permanently erase your local data on that device. This does not automatically remove an existing Drive backup — remove that separately via the app’s backup controls or your Google account.
  • Delete local data / uninstall the app
  • Email us at spika.support@gmail.com for privacy questions or help with data deletion
