Privacy Policy

Effective 26 July 2026 · Operated by Moe Amara (“we”, “us”, “Spika”)

This Privacy Policy explains how Spika, a personal expense tracker mobile app, handles information when you use the app on Google Play (and later on the App Store).

1. Who we are

Spika is currently operated by an individual developer, Moe Amara. There is no separate company entity yet. If that changes, we will update this page.

Contact: github.com/MoeAmara/spika-legal/issues (or the support email shown on our Google Play listing, once published).

2. What Spika is

Spika helps you track personal spending, accounts, categories, and optional precious-metal balances. Features may include voice logging (“Spika Now”), local notifications, market rate lookups, and optional encrypted backup via Google.

3. Information we process

  • Information you enter: transactions, accounts, categories, budgets/income settings, profile display name, and similar finance data you choose to save.
  • Google account information (if you sign in): basic profile details provided by Google Sign-In (such as name, email, and profile photo, when available) to identify your session and enable backup/restore.
  • Device permissions you grant: microphone (for voice entry), notifications, and network access.
  • Technical data on device: app preferences (theme, language, currency, backup settings) stored locally.

Spika is designed so your financial records stay on your device by default. We do not operate a Spika-owned cloud database that stores your transactions for us to read.

4. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: primary storage uses a local database and local preferences.
  • Google Drive App Data (optional / when signed in): Spika can upload an encrypted backup file to the hidden App Data folder associated with your Google account. The backup is encrypted on device before upload. Spika does not host that backup on a Spika server.
  • Market rates: the app may request public market/price data over the internet. Those requests are for rates, not for uploading your transaction history.

5. How we use information

  • Provide and improve the expense-tracking features you use
  • Sign you in with Google and restore or create encrypted backups
  • Convert speech to text locally through the device speech services you allow
  • Send local notifications you enable
  • Show approximate market prices for supported metals/currencies

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal or financial data. We do not share your transaction history with advertisers.

Limited third-party processing happens only as needed to run the app:

  • Google: Sign-In and optional Drive App Data backup/restore
  • Device OS / speech services: if you use microphone features
  • Market data providers / CDN: for public rate lookups
  • App stores: Google Play (and later Apple) for distribution and, in the future, paid subscriptions

7. Subscriptions and payments (future)

Spika is free today. We may introduce paid subscriptions or premium features later. If we do, purchases will be processed by the store (Google Play / App Store). We will not charge you without an in-app confirmation. Store payment details are handled by the store, not entered into Spika’s own forms.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Local data remains until you delete it in-app or uninstall Spika.
  • Encrypted Drive backups remain in your Google App Data until removed by restore/overwrite flows, Google account tools, or app controls that clear them.
  • Uninstalling Spika removes local data on that device; it does not automatically erase a Drive backup that already exists in your Google account.

9. Security

We use on-device storage and encrypted backups for Google Drive App Data. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Protect your device and your Google account with strong locks and up-to-date software.

10. Children

Spika is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the app if you are below that age.

11. International users

The app may be used in different countries. Because data is primarily stored on your device and optionally in your own Google account, processing generally follows where you and your providers (Google / your device) operate.

12. Your choices

  • Use Spika without Google Sign-In (local-only features)
  • Disable backup, notifications, or microphone access in system/app settings
  • Sign out of Google from Settings
  • Delete local data / uninstall the app
  • Contact us via GitHub Issues for privacy questions

13. Changes

We may update this Policy as Spika evolves (for example, when subscriptions or App Store distribution launch). The “Effective date” above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
